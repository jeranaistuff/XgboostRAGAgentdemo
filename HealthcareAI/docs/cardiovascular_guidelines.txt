RIVERSIDE MEDICAL CENTER - CLINICAL GUIDELINES
Document: Cardiovascular Risk Assessment and Prevention Protocol v1.8
Last Updated: December 2024

CARDIOVASCULAR RISK ASSESSMENT
All patients aged 40-79 without existing cardiovascular disease should have 10-year ASCVD risk calculated.
Use the Pooled Cohort Equations (PCE) — available in the EMR under "CV Risk Calculator"

Risk Categories:
- Low risk       : < 5% 10-year ASCVD risk
- Borderline risk: 5% to < 7.5%
- Intermediate   : 7.5% to < 20%
- High risk      : >= 20%

Risk-Enhancing Factors (move patient to higher treatment intensity):
- LDL-C >= 160 mg/dL
- Metabolic syndrome
- Chronic kidney disease
- Inflammatory conditions (rheumatoid arthritis, psoriasis, HIV)
- Premature menopause (before age 40)
- South Asian ancestry
- Persistently elevated triglycerides >= 175 mg/dL

STATIN THERAPY GUIDELINES

High-intensity statins (reduce LDL by >= 50%):
- Atorvastatin 40-80mg daily
- Rosuvastatin 20-40mg daily
Indications: Clinical ASCVD, LDL >= 190 mg/dL, diabetes + high risk, 10-year risk >= 20%

Moderate-intensity statins (reduce LDL by 30-50%):
- Atorvastatin 10-20mg
- Rosuvastatin 10mg
- Simvastatin 20-40mg (note: 80mg dose avoided due to myopathy risk)
Indications: 10-year risk 7.5-20%, diabetes age 40-75 with LDL 70-189 mg/dL

LDL TREATMENT TARGETS
- Known ASCVD (secondary prevention): LDL < 70 mg/dL (ideally < 55 mg/dL)
- Very high risk ASCVD: LDL < 55 mg/dL — add ezetimibe or PCSK9 inhibitor if statin insufficient
- Primary prevention, high risk: LDL < 70 mg/dL
- Primary prevention, intermediate risk: LDL < 100 mg/dL

NON-STATIN THERAPIES
- Ezetimibe 10mg: adds ~20% additional LDL reduction, use if statin alone insufficient
- PCSK9 inhibitors (evolocumab, alirocumab): reduce LDL by 50-60% on top of statins
  Reserved for: familial hypercholesterolemia, very high-risk ASCVD, statin intolerance
  Prior authorization required at Riverside MC — submit via EMR cardiology consult

ASPIRIN FOR PRIMARY PREVENTION
- Age < 40: Not recommended routinely
- Age 40-70 with high ASCVD risk: May be considered (discuss bleeding risk vs benefit)
- Age > 70: Do NOT initiate aspirin for primary prevention (bleeding risk outweighs benefit)
- Secondary prevention (existing CVD): Low-dose aspirin 81mg daily — continue indefinitely

BLOOD PRESSURE TARGETS IN CV DISEASE
- Established coronary artery disease: < 130/80 mmHg
- Heart failure with reduced ejection fraction: ACEI/ARB + beta-blocker + MRA mandatory
- Post-MI: Beta-blocker for minimum 1 year, ACEI/ARB indefinitely

LIFESTYLE COUNSELING (document in EMR at every visit)
- Mediterranean or DASH diet reduces CV events by 25-30%
- Physical activity: 150 min/week moderate or 75 min/week vigorous
- Smoking cessation reduces CV risk by 50% within 1 year
- Obesity management: refer to Riverside MC weight management program (ext. 5512) if BMI > 30
