RIVERSIDE MEDICAL CENTER - CLINICAL GUIDELINES
Document: Type 2 Diabetes Management Protocol v3.2
Last Updated: January 2025

DIAGNOSIS CRITERIA
- Fasting blood glucose >= 126 mg/dL on two separate occasions
- HbA1c >= 6.5%
- 2-hour plasma glucose >= 200 mg/dL during oral glucose tolerance test
- Random plasma glucose >= 200 mg/dL with classic hyperglycemia symptoms

GLYCEMIC TARGETS (Riverside MC specific thresholds)
- HbA1c target: < 7.0% for most adults
- HbA1c target: < 8.0% for elderly patients (>75 years) or those with frequent hypoglycemia
- Fasting glucose target: 80-130 mg/dL
- Post-meal glucose target: < 180 mg/dL (2 hours after eating)

FIRST-LINE TREATMENT
- Metformin 500mg twice daily with meals, titrated to 1000mg twice daily over 4 weeks
- If HbA1c > 9.0% at diagnosis, consider dual therapy from the start
- Lifestyle intervention mandatory: minimum 150 minutes moderate exercise per week
- Dietary referral required within 30 days of diagnosis

SECOND-LINE AGENTS (add if HbA1c not at goal after 3 months)
1. GLP-1 receptor agonists (preferred if cardiovascular disease present): semaglutide, liraglutide
2. SGLT-2 inhibitors (preferred if heart failure or CKD present): empagliflozin, dapagliflozin
3. DPP-4 inhibitors (well tolerated, weight neutral): sitagliptin, saxagliptin
4. Sulfonylureas (low cost, but hypoglycemia risk): glipizide, glimepiride

MONITORING SCHEDULE
- HbA1c every 3 months until stable, then every 6 months
- Fasting lipid panel annually
- Urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio annually
- Foot exam at every visit
- Dilated eye exam annually
- Blood pressure at every visit, target < 130/80 mmHg

HYPOGLYCEMIA MANAGEMENT
- Mild (patient conscious): 15g fast-acting carbohydrates (4oz juice, glucose tablets)
- Severe (patient unconscious): glucagon kit or IV dextrose, call 911
- Threshold for hypoglycemia: blood glucose < 70 mg/dL

CONTRAINDICATIONS FOR METFORMIN
- eGFR < 30 mL/min/1.73m2 (discontinue)
- eGFR 30-45 mL/min/1.73m2 (use with caution, reduce dose)
- IV contrast procedures (hold 48 hours before and after)
- Active liver disease or alcohol abuse
